## Escalation (2026-07-26) — resolved

`tests/integration/m3-trade-turn.test.tsx` and `docs/demos/M3.md` are complete and gate-green
(this card's own owned paths). While building them, discovered that Plan §5's "completes one
locally rendered trade turn with historical remote pricing" cannot be walked through in a
browser today: `packages/ui-shared/src/App.tsx:224` hardcodes `renderFeed(..., 0, ...)`, and
`resolveInformationHorizon` (M3-05) had no production caller anywhere before this card. No M3
card's owned_paths ever included `App.tsx`. Wiring the assembled `travellerPlugin` + real
sector-driven distance into `App.tsx` is a product-behavior change to the app entry point,
which the two-tier method's own review gate names as needing the human gate — not something to
decide silently inside this card. Options, per the standard escalation format:
1. **Amend and re-dispatch**: open a new card (e.g. M3-13) to wire `App.tsx`, blocking M3's
   formal close until it lands.
2. **Accept a documented deviation**: close M3 with this gap named plainly in the Run record,
   the way M2's real-device walkthrough was deferred (Plan §6.2 precedent).

**Decision (2026-07-26): both.** Owner accepted the documented deviation — M3 closes now, gap
named in the Run record below — and separately authorized `docs/tasks/M3-13.md` to wire
`App.tsx`, so the gap gets closed properly rather than left to rot. Full detail in PROJECT.md's
2026-07-26 entry.
